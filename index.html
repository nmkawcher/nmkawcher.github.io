About

Engineer, team lead and teacher.

I'm a Senior Mobile Engineer with deep roots in Flutter and native Android. I like building the parts of an app that nobody sees but everybody feels: a clean architecture, a fast first screen, a release pipeline that doesn't break on Friday.

Most of my work has been in fintech and crypto (digital wallets, remittance, P2P exchange, digital banking), plus SaaS tools for sales teams and sports clubs, and a portable billing system that 50+ organizations use, including Save the Children. How I build

Pick the architecture the project needs.

“Every pattern solves a problem, but not every project has that problem.”From my post on what a senior developer does

Before choosing a structure I look at the project's scope, the team's skill level, the timeline and who will maintain it in two years. At JustGo that meant BLoC with Clean Architecture. For a fast MVP, plain MVVM is often the right call.

  • Layers that earn their placeRepositories and use cases where they reduce risk, not by default.
  • Offline-first where it mattersLocal caching and background sync for field teams and flaky networks.
  • Releases on autopilotFastlane, Azure DevOps and TestFlight so shipping is routine.
  • Measured in productionCrashlytics and performance monitoring after every release.
